Privacy Policy

MoviRank · Last updated: June 30, 2026

MoviRank is operated by Andrew Kirk. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

We collect your email address and a username when you create an account. Your ranked movie list, watchlist, and tags are stored on our servers to enable syncing and social features.

How we use it

To authenticate your account, sync your data across sessions, and display your profile to users you have approved as followers. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication and data storage, and The Movie Database (TMDB) for movie metadata. Neither service receives your personal information beyond what is required for these functions.

Account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and immediate.
