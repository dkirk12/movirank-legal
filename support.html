Account deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, open the app, go to Profile, tap the ⋯ button in the top right, and choose Delete Account. You'll be asked to re-enter your password to confirm.

Reporting & Blocking

To report a user, open their profile, tap ⋯, and choose Report User. To block someone, use ⋯ → Block User. Blocked users are managed under Profile → ⋯ → Blocked Users.
